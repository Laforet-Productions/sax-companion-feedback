# Sax Companion feedback

Tester feedback for the Sax Companion Mac app.

**Issues here are public: anyone can read them.** Don't include anything private (no personal details, and no recordings or scores you don't want shared).

## How to send feedback

1. In Sax Companion, choose **Help > Send Feedback…**.
2. Your browser opens a new issue with the app version, build, commit and macOS version already filled in. Sign in to GitHub if asked (a free account is enough).
3. Say what happened and what you expected, then click **Submit new issue**.

Nothing is sent until you click Submit. The app never posts anything on its own.

You can also open an issue directly: [New issue](https://github.com/Laforet-Productions/sax-companion-feedback/issues/new).
